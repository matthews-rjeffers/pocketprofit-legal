Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

PocketProfitUGCVideoFeed ("the App") is a personal tool that uploads videos from the operator's own computer to the operator's own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Information the App uses

  • TikTok account data: when you log in with TikTok, the App receives an access token, refresh token and your TikTok open ID, and basic profile info (user.info.basic).
  • Videos: video files you choose are sent directly from your computer to TikTok.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the local computer running the App. The App has no server, does not collect analytics, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties other than TikTok.

Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and delete the locally stored token file.
